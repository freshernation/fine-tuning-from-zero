# The AI roles

You get one AI, and it plays several different characters. Which one you open matters
more than what you type into it.

| Role | File | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Planner** | `planner.md` | Week 4, Monday — planning your dataset and your eval |
| **Source finder** | `source-finder.md` | You need a source `SOURCES.md` does not list |
| **Tutor** | `tutor.md` | You are stuck and 20 minutes have passed |
| **Editor** | `editor.md` | Your tests pass and you want to know if the work is any good |
| **Outside view** | `outside-view.md` | Once a week — the question in the week's README |
| **Defend** | `defend.md` | Friday, on your milestone, before your instructor sees it |

## How to use one

Open a fresh chat. Paste the whole role file as your first message, filling in the
`[SQUARE BRACKETS]` at the top. Then talk normally.

**Start a new chat for each session.** A chat that has been running for three days has
forgotten its instructions and is quietly back to being a generic assistant.

## The one thing that ruins this

> "Just fix it for me."

If you type that, you get working code and learn nothing, and in six weeks you will
be unable to explain your own repository. The tutor is built to refuse this. Do not go
around it by opening a plain chat instead — you are only cheating the one person the
course is for.

## What goes in your log

Every role ends its session by printing a `SIGNAL` block. Copy it into
`logs/signal-log.md`. It takes ten seconds and it is how your instructor knows where
to spend the live hour tomorrow.
